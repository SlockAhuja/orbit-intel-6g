# ORBIT-INTEL 6G: Assumptions, Parameter Ranges, and Scientific Limitations

## 1. Physical Assumptions and Scope

1. **Space-Air-Ground Topology**:
   - LEO satellites follow circular orbits at $h = 600\text{ km}$ modeled via Keplerian mechanics and SGP4 orbital kinematics.
   - HAPS is stationed in the lower stratosphere ($20\text{ km}$ altitude) with negligible horizontal drift ($<5\text{ m/s}$).
   - UAV operates in low-altitude airspace ($300\text{ m}$) over designated service cells.
   - Terrestrial Base Stations have fixed towers with $30\text{ m}$ height.

2. **Channel Modeling Scope**:
   - Channel calculations comply with 3GPP TR 38.811 (Release 17/18) and ITU-R P.618-13.
   - Path loss includes Free-Space Path Loss, atmospheric gas attenuation (ITU-R P.676), rain fade (ITU-R P.618), and log-normal shadow fading.
   - Fast fading is modeled via Rician fading for Line-of-Sight (LoS) links (K-factor $K \in [6, 15]\text{ dB}$) and Rayleigh fading for Non-Line-of-Sight (NLoS) terrestrial multipath.
   - Carrier frequency: $f_c = 2.0\text{ GHz}$ (S-band) for mobile NTN service links and $f_c = 28.0\text{ GHz}$ (Ka-band) for feeder/high-capacity links.

3. **Software & Computational Boundaries**:
   - Simulator runs discrete-time synchronized epochs ($\delta t = 1.0\text{ s}$) with event-driven packet delivery models.
   - The implementation is CPU-first, designed for high computational efficiency, reproducibility, and transparent scientific auditability without requiring multi-GPU clusters.

## 3. Methodological Limitations

1. **Not a Full-Stack 3GPP Testbed**:
   - The simulator models physical, link, and orchestration layers accurately according to 3GPP/ITU-R standards, but does not execute low-level MAC bit-level parity, LDPC encoding/decoding, or full ASN.1 RRC protocol state machines.
2. **Synthetic Channel Realizations**:
   - While based directly on empirical ITU-R P.618 and 3GPP TR 38.811 equations, datasets are generated by high-fidelity mathematical simulation rather than live satellite hardware telemetry.
3. **Orbital Ephemeris Approximation**:
   - SGP4 orbital propagation provides high fidelity, but real-world orbital perturbation (J2, solar radiation pressure, atmospheric drag) causes slight long-term secular drift not modeled over short simulation runs ($<1\text{ hour}$).
