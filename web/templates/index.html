Core Innovation: CAPRO Framework
Confidence-Aware Predictive Reliability Orchestration (CAPRO) integrates physics-grounded 3GPP TR 38.811 / TR 38.821 channel propagation and ITU-R P.618 rain fading with dual-uncertainty conformal link forecasting. CAPRO decomposes prediction risk into epistemic model variance and aleatoric channel flutter, preemptively triggering risk-adjusted multi-tier handovers before physical link drops occur.
Heterogeneous SAGIN Architecture
A real-time four-tier Space-Air-Ground Integrated Network:
  • LEO Space Tier: Circular Keplerian orbits (500–600 km altitude, 7.56 km/s, 53° inclination).
  • HAPS Stratospheric Tier: High-altitude pseudo-satellite relay (20 km altitude, wide-area footprint).
  • UAV Tactical Tier: Low-altitude localized airborne loiter (300 m altitude).
  • Terrestrial Ground Tier: 3GPP gNodeB base stations (30 m tower height, low latency).
